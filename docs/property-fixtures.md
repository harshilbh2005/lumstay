# Property fixture sources

Casa Serein is a fictional property used to exercise the LumaStay interface. Its operational details are a transparent composite adapted from public, first-party Ravello hospitality and destination references; they do not represent live inventory or bookable terms.

| Fixture area | Source | Adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heated infinity pool, terrace dining, family provisions, pets, check-in/out, seasonal cancellation structure, and transfer patterns | [Hotel Caruso official factsheet](https://pdfs.belmond.com/Belmond_Hotel_Caruso_Factsheet.pdf) | Condensed into the facilities and house-policy ledger without copying room inventory or property claims. |
| Elevated Ravello setting and destination character | [Visit Ravello](https://www.ravello.com/) | The published elevation is retained; destination language is paraphrased. |
| Arrival by road, Salerno rail and sea connections, the 7 km Amalfi link, and public parking guidance | [Fondazione Ravello arrival guide](https://www.fondazioneravello.com/la-fondazione/come-arrivare-a-ravello/) | Travel routes are simplified into guest-facing practical notes. |
| Pool, spa, dining, and on-request transfer patterns | [Palazzo Avino](https://www.palazzoavino.com/) | Used as a secondary cross-check for the service mix of an upscale Ravello stay. |

The Casa Serein name, twelve-room count, editorial voice, pricing, rating, and imagery remain illustrative product fixtures. Exact availability, cancellation charges, room-specific occupancy, accessibility, and live operating dates must eventually come from a real property data source.
